    humanconnect.online 💚

    The live map of real-world meetups

    See something you like? Tap it and join. The pin grows as more people join, so good plans become impossible to miss.

    Want to start something? Tap anywhere on the map, compose your event from the word list, and give it up to a week. When time runs out, it vanishes forever.

    Need a hand instead? In the same sheet, switch to Need help and put a request on the map — a lift, a ladder, blood, an hour of tutoring. Those pins are square, and people tap I'm coming.

    • No accounts, no chat, no profiles — just a map.
    • Names come from a fixed word list, so the map stays friendly.
    • Joining is anonymous — it simply counts you in.

    Everything here is posted by the community, and anyone can post. Meet in public places, take someone with you, never hand over money or documents, and in a real emergency call 112 (women's helpline 1091). HumanConnect only shows the map and takes no responsibility for what happens at events or requests.
